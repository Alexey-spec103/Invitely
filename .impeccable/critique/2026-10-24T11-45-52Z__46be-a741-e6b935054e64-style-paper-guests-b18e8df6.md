## Fixed since this assessment, independently verified live

**Canvas editor's undo history was keystroke-grained, not edit-grained (A's P1).** `ElementToolbar.tsx`'s text/number inputs (`fontSize`, `lineHeight`, `letterSpacing`, QR caption, animation duration) fired `onUpdate` on every keystroke with no debounce, and each of `PaperConstructor.tsx`'s three history-mutation functions (`useCardCanvas.updateFrame`, `updateFrontCanvasFrame`, `updateBackCanvasFrame`) pushed a new history entry on every call. A confirmed live: selecting the font-size field (72), typing "90", then pressing Ctrl+Z once left the field at "9" (the first keystroke's intermediate state) instead of restoring 72 -- the single most natural "oops" gesture a host has was broken.

Fixed by threading an optional `coalesceKey` parameter through the whole chain (ElementToolbar input -> `onUpdate` -> `CanvasFrameEditor.updateElement` -> `onUpdateFrame` -> PaperConstructor's three history functions), combined with a new `shouldCoalesceEdit()` helper that merges same-field edits within an 800ms window into one history entry instead of pushing a new one per keystroke. Applied to all five affected inputs (`fontSize`, `lineHeight`, `letterSpacing`, QR caption, animation duration).

Re-verified live after the fix, on the Front invitation canvas: selected the "Amelia" text element (fontSize 72), typed "90" (single history entry, field read "90"), pressed Ctrl+Z once -> field read "72" (the true original, not an intermediate keystroke state). Ctrl+Shift+Z (redo) correctly returned to "90". A second Ctrl+Z correctly returned to "72" for cleanup. Arrow-key nudge granularity (one press = one undo step) confirmed unaffected by B in the same session, independently.

## Priority Issues

- **[P2] Concurrent-session edits silently clobber each other, with zero visibility.** A reproduced this by accident mid-review: a second tab editing the same event's Guests tab overwrote a guest they had just added, with no warning, no merge, no "someone else is editing" indicator. Real, demonstrated silent-data-loss risk for any couple co-editing from two devices -- not hypothetical. Judged out of scope for a quick fix (needs real conflict-detection/collaboration infrastructure); not addressed this round.
- **[P2] Error banners (`formError`/`downloadError`/`emailError`) still have no retry affordance.** Confirmed unchanged across GuestManager, AttendeeList, InvitationSentBadge, SendInviteMenu, EditGuestForm. Flagged across rounds 7-9 now; not addressed this round.
- **[P3] Guests' anchor-nav pills don't reflect actual scroll position** once the user scrolls past the clicked section or navigates away and back -- a deliberate plain-anchor-links tradeoff from when the nav was added, not a regression.
- **[P3] Status-count/list mismatch during optimistic guest removal** -- immediately after clicking Remove (inside the 5-second undo window), the status pills still reflect the pre-removal count while the list body already says "No guests match this filter." New observation this round, not addressed.
- **[P3] Native `window.confirm()` for revert/removal actions** breaks the product's otherwise-consistent modal/toast visual language with unstylable browser chrome. Pre-existing, deliberate choice, not a new regression.
