## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Paper's canvas editor is cramped at its embedded size.** Measured ~130px rendered card width inside a ~230px-wide strip, with the floating element toolbar clipped (horizontal scroll needed to reach alignment/style controls). Root cause: the page's `max-w-3xl` wrapper plus a `[minmax(0,160px)_1fr_minmax(0,260px)]` grid leaves the editor only the leftover middle column. **Fix options**: let the editor break out of `max-w-3xl` (full-bleed or a wider dedicated width) when active, or switch to a full-screen/modal editing mode on "Edit text", matching the room the separate Site canvas editor already gets.
- **[P1] Theme cards commit the live theme change on a single, unconfirmed click.** Reproduced live, twice: a click aimed at something else landed on a card's caption and switched the active theme. The undo toast is damage control, not prevention, for the single highest-stakes action on the tab. **Fix options**: require the click to land on a dedicated "select" affordance rather than the full card+caption area, or require a second confirming action before committing.
- **[P2] No keyboard path to Style's or Guests' search.** B's direct test confirmed `/` (or any key) does nothing -- both are mouse-only to even start typing a search. **Fix**: bind `/` to focus the respective search input on both tabs.
- **[P3] "Add a design slot" modal duplicates the entire page-level gallery verbatim** inside a modal meant for a lighter "shortlist a candidate" action -- reads as "the whole page reopened in a box" rather than a purpose-built picker.
