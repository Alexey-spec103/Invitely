## Priority Issues

**[P1] "ACTIVE" theme badge still fails contrast — the exact bug class, one file missed**
Why it matters: confirmed independently by both the design review (live-verified) and the detector (DOM-measured, identical 2.82:1 value) — this is the one piece of text telling a host which card is actually live, rendering near-illegible on a white card.
Fix: `DesignSlotsPanel.module.css`'s `.cardBadge` (and its sibling `.addCard:hover`/`.historyBtn:hover`, same raw-accent pattern) — swap `var(--dash-accent, #ff6b45)` to `var(--dash-accent-text, #c2410c)`, same fix already applied everywhere else.
Tabs: Style.

**[P1] Paper tab: "Revert to default" has no confirm or undo**
Why it matters: one click on the front-invitation card permanently discards a host's custom canvas design — the identical stakes profile as the theme switch that *was* given a 6-second undo toast this round, and the guest-delete that already has one. The app clearly knows this pattern; this one control was missed.
Fix: gate behind `window.confirm()` (matching the existing `AttendeeList` pattern) or extend the same undo-toast shape already proven three times over.
Tabs: Paper.

**[P2] Systemic `text-gray-500` near-miss, 16 occurrences across all three tabs**
Why it matters: `rgb(106,114,130)` on the dashboard's light card background sits at exactly 4.25:1 — a small, consistent miss of the 4.5:1 AA floor, recurring identically on Style's header subtitle, Paper's description text, and Guests' section subtitles. It's the single largest remaining contrast bucket now that the louder failures are gone.
Fix: same swap pattern already used elsewhere (`text-gray-500` → `text-gray-600`), one component class, cheap to close out.
Tabs: Style, Paper, Guests.

**[P2] Style tab: the sidebar as a whole is still dense**
Why it matters: the specific 20-item category list was correctly collapsed, but the sidebar sits inside three *other* ungated filter rows (entry row: 7, style row now 6 by default, season row: 5) — 18 buttons simultaneously visible before touching the grid. The named finding was fixed at the scope it was measured, not at the scope of the underlying ≤4-per-group principle.
Fix: apply the same collapse-to-N-plus-toggle treatment already built for the category list to the entry row and season row.
Tabs: Style.

**[P2] Guests tab: returning hosts scroll past a first-time-user content stack every visit**
Why it matters: share-link banner, onboarding banner, a "short on time" tip, the full add-guest form, and two bulk-send buttons all render above the guest list itself — appropriate for a first visit, repetitive for a host checking RSVPs for the twentieth time.
Fix: collapse the onboarding-tier banners once the list is non-empty, or move the add-form behind a "+ Add guest" affordance the way the Style tab already uses a modal for its own "add" flow.
Tabs: Guests.
